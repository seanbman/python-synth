# Getting Started

Your first sound should take less than a minute. This page walks through the small set of things to check before you play.

## Connect a controller

Connect your MIDI keyboard or controller to your computer before opening Synthepyzer. USB MIDI controllers are usually ready as soon as the operating system recognizes them.

Open the controller menu and choose the device you want to use. If you have more than one controller connected, look for the name printed on the device or the name shown by your operating system.

## Choose a starting sound

Begin with a preset that resembles what you want to play. A soft pad is useful for learning the filter and envelope. A bright lead makes pitch and modulation easy to hear. A bass sound gives you a clear sense of the instrument’s low end.

You can change a sound without losing the original preset. Explore freely, and save the versions you want to keep as you learn what each control does.

## Play your first notes

Play a few single notes, then try a short chord. Listen for three things:

- How quickly the sound begins when you press a key.
- How long it continues after you release the key.
- Which part of the sound feels too bright, too soft, or too still.

These observations give you a useful starting point for the filter and envelope controls.

## If you do not hear anything

Check that the controller is selected and that your computer’s audio output is available. Try a different preset and play one note at a time. The MIDI activity indicator should respond when you press a key; if it does not, return to [MIDI Controllers](#/midi-controllers) and check the connection.

Once you can play a note, the best next step is [Playing](#/playing).
