# Make sound, your way

Synthepyzer is a focused software instrument for playing, shaping, and exploring synthesizer sounds from your computer. Connect a MIDI controller, choose a starting sound, and use the instrument’s controls to make it your own.

The manual is written for the moment when you want to make music—not when you want to read a technical specification. Start with the basics, then follow the parts that match the way you like to play.

## The idea

Synthepyzer begins with a simple subtractive-synthesis workflow. Sound starts with bright, harmonically rich waveforms. You combine them, shape their tone with a filter, and give the sound movement with envelopes and modulation.

![A sound moving from oscillator through harmonics, filtering, and an amplitude envelope](assets/signal-path.png)

You do not need to understand synthesis before you begin. Every control is there to answer a musical question: is the sound brighter or darker, softer or sharper, steady or moving?

## Your first session

The basic path is short:

- Connect a MIDI keyboard or other controller.
- Choose a sound that gives you a useful starting point.
- Play a few notes and listen for what you want to change.
- Shape the sound with the filter, envelope, and modulation controls.
- Save the result when it becomes something you want to hear again.

> A good first patch is one that makes you want to play another note. You can learn the details as you go.
